Professional Tax

TDS on salary

FAQ

Questions HR teams and founders ask

Something we haven't covered? Book a demo and ask us directly.

Is Humora suitable for small companies?

Yes. Starter is built for small teams of up to 25 employees. You get the employee directory, attendance, leave and statutory payroll from day one.

Can I manage multiple companies or entities?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. Humora is multi-tenant, so each entity gets its own workspace, with central reporting across them. Talk to us and we will plan it with you.

Can employees have separate accounts?

Yes. Every person signs in with their own work email. Company admin, HR admin, manager and employee each see only the screens their role needs.

What is a tenant slug?

Every company gets its own workspace, identified by a short tenant slug. You enter it with your work email and password when you sign in, so you land in your own company's data.

Does it handle Indian statutory payroll?

Yes. EPF, ESI, Professional Tax and TDS are calculated as part of every monthly pay run.

Can I migrate my existing employee data?

Yes, as a paid add-on. We can bring in employee records, salary structures, opening leave balances and past payroll. It is priced on request, depending on how much data you have and where it lives.

Does it work on mobile?

Humora runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Employees clock in and apply for leave from their phones every day. There is no separate native app yet.

Do you provide onboarding?

Every account gets a setup guide and demo accounts to explore. If you want us to set up your departments, salary structures and leave policies for you, assisted onboarding is available as a paid add-on.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. There is no lock-in. If you pay yearly, your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your data is not deleted. Your employees, attendance and leave records stay safe while you pick a plan, and full access comes back as soon as you subscribe.

Is employee data secure?

Each company's data stays in its own tenant. Access is by role, sensitive IDs show only the last 4 digits, finalised payroll is locked, and every important change is in the audit log.
